## 0.12.3

* Published as `treebars_flutter_sdk`. The same release was first published as `treebars_flutter`, which is
  discontinued in its favour: change the dependency and the import to `package:treebars_flutter_sdk/treebars_flutter_sdk.dart`.
* First release: a bridge over the Treebars Android and iOS SDKs 0.12.3. Events, screens, sign-in and sign-out,
  push tokens and opens, remote config, in-app messages drawn by the native SDKs, the in-app inbox, the
  notification centre and its topics, app actions, opt-out and forgetting a device.
